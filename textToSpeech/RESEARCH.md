# Research Notes for Text To Speech (TTS)

## Overview
This document contains research notes and references that have been used or may be useful in the development and improvement of the Text To Speech (TTS) project.

## Research Topics

### 1. Text to Speech Synthesis
- Techniques and algorithms for converting text to natural sounding speech.
- Analysis of current state-of-the-art TTS models such as Tacotron, WaveNet, and FastSpeech.

### 2. Acoustic Modelling
- Methods for capturing the nuances of human speech including intonation, stress, and rhythm.
- Use of neural networks for acoustic feature prediction.

### 3. Linguistic Features
- Incorporation of phonemes, prosody, and context for better synthesis.
- Language-specific adaptations.

### 4. Deep Learning Frameworks
- Comparison of TensorFlow, PyTorch, and other frameworks for efficient model training.

### 5. Evaluation Metrics
- Objective and subjective measures for TTS quality including MCD (Mel Cepstral Distortion), PESQ (Perceptual Evaluation of Speech Quality), and MOS (Mean Opinion Score).

## References
- "Tacotron: Towards End-to-End Speech Synthesis," Wang et al.
- "WaveNet: A Generative Model for Raw Audio," van den Oord et al.
- Relevant research papers and articles from IEEE, arXiv, and others.

## Future Work
- Integration of multilingual support.
- Real-time TTS applications.
- Custom voice generation and adaptation.
